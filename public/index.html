YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Before you analyze

Your resume text and job description are sent to this app’s server and Google Gemini for analysis. Remove contact details and other personal information you don’t need to include.

The app does not intentionally store raw resumes or job descriptions. Hosting and AI providers process requests under their own policies. Check the Gemini terms for your account before using sensitive or third-party resumes.

PDF extraction happens locally in your browser; the extracted text is sent only when you click Analyze. Optional history stores the report, including quoted resume excerpts, on this device. Clear it from Saved reports, particularly on shared devices.

Feedback supports your own review. It is not a hiring decision, verified ATS score or employment guarantee.
